# NOTES — One Word for Your Wi-Fi

## The word

**Ghosting** (8 characters, one word, within the 1–20 character gate).

## Rationale

The judge weighs, in order: on-subject fit, instant humour, truth about
how home Wi-Fi actually behaves, freshness of angle, and working as a
single word with no context needed.

- **On subject:** it describes Wi-Fi as the entrant lives it — the
  connection itself, not the router or the bill.
- **Funny at once:** "ghosting" is a dating word; applying it to a
  network is an instant, recognisable joke that needs no explanation.
- **True:** the universal home-Wi-Fi experience is not slowness, it is
  sudden disappearance — full bars, then nothing, mid-call. Everyone
  has lived it.
- **Fresh angle:** the obvious entries are "Buffering", "Slow",
  "Loading", "Laggy". Ghosting sidesteps the speed cliché entirely and
  picks the vanishing act instead.
- **Works alone:** one common word, spelled as everyone expects, and it
  doubles as a network name the sponsor can actually adopt — which is
  the point of the bounty.

## Alternatives considered and rejected

- Buffering / Loading — the words everyone reaches for; zero freshness.
- Slow — true but not funny, and the most obvious entry in the field.
- Flaky — true and short, but reads as a complaint about the router,
  not a joke; less funny than Ghosting.
- Temperamental — accurate, but needs a beat to land and is less funny.
- MIA — too cryptic as a standalone word; fails "no context needed".

## Verification

`python3 main.py` writes exactly `Ghosting` (8 bytes, no trailing
newline) to `/output/entry.txt`, passing the gate.
